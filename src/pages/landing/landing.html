FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Guru 2 u read a person?

An AI trained on face reading, palmistry, astrology and conversation analysis looks at what you give it and writes a personality profile, a 1–100% judgment and advice on how to deal with that person.

Are my photos and recordings stored?

No. Photos and recordings are used only to create the reading and are not kept. Your readings themselves are saved to your account so you can find them again.

Which languages can I use?

Readings can be written and read aloud in 21 languages, including Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu and Assamese. Read-aloud uses your phone's voices.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Buy a pack of 30 readings for ₹699 and use them whenever you like. They never expire.

How do I delete my account?

From the app (Profile → Delete account) or on our account deletion page.
